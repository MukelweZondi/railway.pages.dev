FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does delivery take?

Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days. Delays can occur during busy periods or due to courier circumstances.

Do you deliver throughout South Africa?

RAILWAY intends to offer delivery across South Africa. Available delivery options and charges should be confirmed at checkout.

Can I cancel an order after placing it?

Orders may not be cancellable once placed. Please check your product, colour, size and delivery details carefully before confirming. Your statutory rights remain unaffected.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

RAILWAY does not offer refunds for change-of-mind returns. Eligible regular-price items may qualify for an exchange under the Returns & Exchanges policy. Applicable consumer rights are not excluded.

Can sale items be exchanged?

Sale items are generally final sale, subject to applicable consumer-protection law and your statutory rights.

What if I receive the wrong item?

Contact Customer Care with your order details and information about the item received so the issue can be assessed.

What if my item is damaged or defective?

Contact Customer Care with your order details and clear photographs, where possible. The issue will be assessed and an appropriate remedy provided in line with applicable consumer law.

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Change of mind

RAILWAY does not offer refunds for change-of-mind returns. Eligible regular-price items may be considered for an exchange, subject to this policy and stock availability.

Exchange conditions

Items requested for exchange should be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with original tags and packaging where applicable. Contact Customer Care before sending an item back to confirm the process and applicable timeframe.

Sale items

Sale items are generally final sale and not eligible for change-of-mind exchanges, subject to applicable consumer-protection law.

Wrong, damaged or defective items

Contact Customer Care with your order details and relevant photographs. RAILWAY will assess the issue and provide an appropriate remedy as required by applicable law.

Contact us first

Please do not send an item back before contacting Customer Care for return or exchange instructions.
